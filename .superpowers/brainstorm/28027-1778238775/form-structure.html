How should the form be structured?

This shapes the whole interaction — choose what feels right for your audience

A

Multi-step wizard

Split into 3–4 steps. One topic per screen (contact info → your setup → your goals). Progress bar at top. Feels like a conversation, not a form. Each step validates before advancing.

B

Enhanced single-page

Keep everything on one page but add animated field focus states, floating labels, real-time validation feedback, and micro-interactions. Feels polished and premium without changing the structure.

C

Conversational / chat-style

Questions appear one at a time as if being asked by a person. User answers and presses Enter (or clicks Next). Very low-friction, feels modern. Higher completion rates for qualifying forms.
